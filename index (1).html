Shopping

    Raw weights unless the food says cooked. Updates to whichever week you have selected.

    How to weigh food and yourself

    Use a digital kitchen scale in grams. Meat, fish and vegetables are raw weights; rice, quinoa, barley, pasta, lentils and beans are cooked weights.

    Weigh yourself every morning after the bathroom, before eating. The first week usually drops 4–8 lb, mostly water. After that, 2–3 lb a week is the realistic range.

    Free: water, black coffee, tea, sparkling water, herbs, spices, garlic, lemon, vinegar, mustard, hot sauce. Alcohol isn't in the plan.

    When to adjust

    If your 7-day average drops less than 1.5 lb a week for two weeks in a row (after week 2), drop the afternoon snack. Don't go below about 1,850 kcal without your doctor.

    If you're losing more than 4 lb a week after week 2, feel dizzy, or workouts are falling apart, add the snack back.

    Sleep under 6 hours or heavy legs: skip the evening session, not the morning one.

    Cholesterol and safety

    Oats, barley, beans and lentils supply soluble fiber; salmon twice a week; nuts, seeds and olive oil replace butter, cheese and red or processed meat. One whole egg twice a week.

    Get a quick check-up before starting, and a repeat lipid panel near the end. If you take a statin, ask about grapefruit (none is in this plan).

    Running: any sharp or worsening pain in shins, knees, feet or Achilles means repeat the previous week. Get fitted for running shoes first.
